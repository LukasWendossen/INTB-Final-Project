What I did

Tie each course concept to a specific action you took, not just 'I used it.'

  • 1. What happened: you missed the midterm on 10/11 and got a one-time extension; then the Legal Analysis upload got locked.
  • 2. What you did: designed School HQ, a headquarters for your semester with one folder per course, a master tracker, a School calendar synced to your phone, weekly Sunday reviews, and an AI chief of staff.
  • 3. Which course learning you applied: Ch 10 control mechanisms. Output controls (grades and deadlines tracked), bureaucratic controls (rules: book CASA exams early, submit 24 hours ahead), information systems (calendar and dashboards).
  • 4. Why it fit: three courses act like subsidiaries with different platforms and rules, and they needed central coordination with local flexibility.
  • 5. The result: Legal Analysis 94, Quiz 2 100, INTB total up from 57.9% to 68.3% in a week, every exam booked on time.
  • 6. What you learned, including what didn't go as planned and what you'd do differently.
  • Communication application: how you prepared for and handled the 10/21 meeting with Prof. Wengelnik.
