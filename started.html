Where I started

Claude's draft from your interview answers (10/29). Still missing: one or two lines on your soft skills and how you saw yourself as a team member or future leader. Rewrite it in your own voice, then delete this box.

When the semester started in August, I was a little distracted as a student. I was doing the work, but I wasn't as dedicated as I am now, and I wasn't putting real time or energy into building a system that would set me up to succeed.

I did bring real strengths. Since high school I've had a note-taking and study-set method that works for me, and I've adjusted it every semester since, including this one. When I sat down to learn something, I could learn it well.

Where I was weak was everything around the studying. Before School HQ, my system was opening Canvas, comparing it with my calendar, and typing each deadline in by hand, one at a time. It worked as long as nothing went wrong, and it meant my whole semester depended on me remembering to check. I also didn't know that basic Git commands, HTML, CSS and the Terminal could do much of that work for me, or how much more organized they would make me on my computer.

The assumption I brought into this course was that good study habits were enough, and that I didn't need a system around them. If I could learn the material, I figured the deadlines and planning across three courses would take care of themselves.

In August, I knew how to study. I didn't yet know how to run a semester.
