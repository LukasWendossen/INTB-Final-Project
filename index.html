Introduction: what is my story?

Claude's draft from your interview answers (10/29). Rewrite it in your own voice, then delete this box.

In October, the deadline for my INTB 3355 midterm came and went. Something heavy was happening in my personal life, and it hit at the worst possible time. The midterm was the most visible thing that slipped through the cracks that month, but it wasn't the only one.

Looking back, the problem wasn't that I couldn't do the work. It was that I had no system around the work. My deadlines lived in a calendar I updated by hand, my courses lived on different platforms, and nothing connected what I meant to do with what actually happened each day. When life got heavy, there was nothing in place to catch what I dropped.

That turned out to be a business problem, not just a personal one. In Chapter 10, Organizational Design and Control, I learned that a multinational's strategy only works when it has a structure that fits it and controls that keep every unit on track. So I started running my semester like a headquarters. I built School HQ, a home base that oversees my three courses the way a parent company oversees its subsidiaries, with a tracker, a shared calendar, weekly reviews and an AI chief of staff as its control systems.

This matters to me beyond one class. I'm working toward a corporate career, and the organizations I want to join run on exactly this: clear structure, regular reporting, and people who can communicate across different contexts, which is the second thread of this project, drawn from Hall's and Trompenaars' frameworks. This site follows that journey: where I started, what I learned, what I built, how I've grown, and where I'm going next.
